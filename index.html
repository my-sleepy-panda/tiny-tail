How to play

Select two tiles that have the same number or consecutive numbers.

A successful merge adds their values, introduces a new low-number tile, and sorts the board from largest to smallest.

Land exactly on the active target to complete it. The game ends when no valid pair remains.

Reach Pro mode to unlock a more relaxed pace and a friendlier mix of new tiles for longer runs.
